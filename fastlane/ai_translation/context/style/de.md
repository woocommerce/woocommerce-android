# German style notes

- Use the **informal "Du"** form throughout. This is the Automattic-wide
  convention; do **not** default to the formal "Sie" even though the model
  might prefer it.
- Prefer concise nouns over long compound constructions when both fit the
  screen.
- Keep brand names (WooCommerce, Woo, WordPress.com, Jetpack) untranslated.
