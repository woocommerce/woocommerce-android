# Hebrew style notes

- **Prefer the infinitive form** for action labels (buttons, menu items) rather
  than defaulting to the male gender. The default male gender is the most
  common model output and is increasingly seen as outdated; the infinitive
  form is gender-neutral and is the convention the human translators have
  consistently used.
- Keep brand names (WooCommerce, Woo, WordPress.com, Jetpack) untranslated.
- RTL punctuation: use the source punctuation; do not invert.
